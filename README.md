1. Setup & Analytics

• Google Search Console (GSC): Submit your site and monitor indexing errors, search queries, and coverage.
• Bing Webmaster Tools: Set up Bing and check its AI performance reports for Bing Copilot and AI summaries.
• Google Analytics 4 (GA4): Track organic traffic, user behavior, and conversion metrics.
• XML Sitemap: Create and submit a dynamic XML sitemap so search engines can easily find all your pages.
• Robots.txt: Ensure your robots.txt file is properly configured to allow crawlers (including major AI bots) and block private pages.

2. Technical SEO

• Mobile-Friendliness: Make sure your theme and layout work smoothly on mobile phones and tablets.
• Page Speed: Test your site using Google PageSpeed Insights to improve loading times.
• SSL Certificate (HTTPS): Verify that your site runs on a secure HTTPS connection.
• Fix Broken Links & Redirects: Resolve 404 errors and broken internal or external links, using proper 301 redirects.
• Canonical Tags: Implement self-referencing canonical URLs to prevent duplicate content issues.

3. Keyword Research & Search Intent

• Search Intent: Match your page format and content type (blog post, product page, landing page) to what searchers actually want.
• Keyword Research: Identify primary target keywords and relevant semantic or topical terms.
• Topical Authority: Build clusters of related content around your core subject matter rather than isolated articles.

4. On-Page & Content Optimization

• Title Tags: Write descriptive, unique title tags under 60 characters with your primary keyword front-loaded.
• H1 and Subheadings: Use a single clear H1 tag containing your main topic, followed by structured H2/H3 subheadings.
• Meta Descriptions: Craft compelling meta descriptions (around 150 characters) to improve click-through rates (CTR).
• Content Quality & Helpful Content: Provide original, well-researched, and up-to-date information that demonstrates firsthand experience and fact-checking.
• Image Optimization: Compress image file sizes, use descriptive file names, and add relevant alt text.
• Internal Linking: Link naturally to related internal pages to pass authority and help users navigate.

5. Off-Page SEO & Link Building

• Backlink Acquisition: Earn quality, relevant backlinks from reputable sites in your industry.
• Unlinked Mentions: Track brand mentions across the web and reach out to request a link where appropriate.
• Competitor Analysis: Identify backlink gaps to see where competitors are getting links that you aren't.

6. AI & Modern Search Readiness

• AI Overview & ChatGPT Optimization: Structure content clearly using semantic HTML so LLMs (like Google Gemini, ChatGPT, and Perplexity) can parse and retrieve your data.
• Freshness Signals: Display clear publication and last-updated dates on your articles, as AI systems heavily favor recent content.
